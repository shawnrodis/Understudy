Fixes the broken v0.1.3 build and gives the Status tab both halves of pairing.

**Fixed**
- **The build.** v0.1.3 used `Clipboard` without importing `package:flutter/services.dart`, which broke `flutter analyze` and the Windows build — that is why v0.1.3 shipped no installer. This release builds.
- Four analyzer warnings that would also have failed CI (`flutter analyze` treats warnings as errors): two null checks that could never be false once the `connected` flag promotes its locals, and two redundant `!` in the Tailscale subtitle, now written as an explicit null test.

**Changed**
- The **Controller connection** card now shows the engine's real listen address from `connection.json` instead of the Tailscale IP. When Tailscale connects after the engine has already started, the engine is bound to loopback, and quoting the Tailscale IP handed a controller an address nothing answers on.
- The **pairing card** keeps the bearer token, the reachability warning, and one-paste connection details, and drops its now-duplicate address row. The address and the token are still never displayed together — they only leave the app together through the explicitly labelled copy button.

**Added**
- **Copy connection details for the AI.** One click puts a ready-to-use block on the clipboard: base URL, bearer token, the `Authorization` header, reachability, what 401 and 501 mean, the session workflow (`/status` → `/monitors` → screenshot → per-monitor coordinates → input), the endpoint list, and where the full contract lives.
- **Copy token** next to the existing reveal toggle, so pairing no longer requires putting the token on screen first.

**Version**
- 0.1.4 across the engine, app, installer and docs.

**Installer**
- `Understudy_Setup_0.1.4.exe`
